## 2. Test Coverage Areas

The automated tests cover:

- **Security:** security-related validation and access controls.
- **Workspace management:** company workspace creation and selection.
- **Role-based access control:** Owner, Staff, Viewer, and platform administrator permissions.
- **Tenant isolation:** company data separation and rejection of cross-company operations.
- **API behavior:** customer and invoice operations and access restrictions.
- **Database constraints:** unique customer emails and invoice numbers within a company, and valid company-customer relationships.
- **Pagination and search:** paginated records and invoice status filtering.
- **Subscription plans:** plan-related rules and limits.
- **Serialization and interface behavior:** data serialization and shell-view behavior.

## 4. Expected Database Error Logs

During end-to-end testing, Prisma printed error messages for deliberately invalid database operations, including:

- Creating a duplicate customer email within the same company.
- Creating a duplicate invoice number within the same company.
- Creating an invoice referencing a customer belonging to another company.

These errors were expected during database constraint and tenant-isolation tests. The relevant test suites passed.
